# Task F.66 — Percentage Price Oscillator (PPO) Panel

## Overview
Percentage Price Oscillator — a percentage-normalised MACD that measures the
distance between two EMAs as a fraction of the slower EMA, making it
comparable across securities with different price levels.

  PPO       = (EMA(12) − EMA(26)) / EMA(26) × 100
  Signal    = EMA(9, PPO)
  Histogram = PPO − Signal

Score (0–100):
  35 pts  PPO > Signal (bullish crossover zone)
  35 pts  PPO > 0 (fast EMA above slow EMA)
  30 pts  percentile rank of PPO in 252-bar rolling window × 30
