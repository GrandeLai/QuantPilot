# Task F.68 — Klinger Volume Oscillator (KVO) Panel

## Overview
Klinger Volume Oscillator — combines price trend and volume to measure long-term
money flow vs short-term fluctuations.

  DM[i]  = High[i] + Low[i] + Close[i]  (daily midpoint proxy)
  Trend  = +1 if DM[i] > DM[i-1] else −1
  VF     = Volume × |2×(dm/cumRange − 1)| × Trend × 100
  KVO    = EMA(34, VF) − EMA(55, VF)
  Signal = EMA(13, KVO)

Score (0–100):
  40 pts  KVO > Signal (money flow turning bullish)
  30 pts  KVO > 0 (net accumulation)
  30 pts  percentile rank of KVO in 252-bar window × 30
