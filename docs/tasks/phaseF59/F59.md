# Task F.59 — Ichimoku Cloud Panel

## Overview
Ichimoku Kinko Hyo — complete cloud indicator measuring trend direction, momentum, and support/resistance.

  Tenkan-sen (9)  = (highest_high + lowest_low) / 2  over 9 bars
  Kijun-sen (26)  = (highest_high + lowest_low) / 2  over 26 bars
  Senkou A        = (Tenkan + Kijun) / 2  (plotted 26 bars ahead)
  Senkou B (52)   = (highest_high + lowest_low) / 2  over 52 bars  (plotted 26 bars ahead)
  Chikou Span     = Close  plotted 26 bars back

Score composite (0–100):
  - Price above/below Kumo (cloud): 40 pts
  - Tenkan vs Kijun cross: 30 pts
  - Senkou A vs Senkou B (cloud color): 30 pts
