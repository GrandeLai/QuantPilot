# Task F.65 — Chande Momentum Oscillator (CMO) Panel

## Overview
Chande Momentum Oscillator — measures raw momentum by comparing the sum of up-closes
to the sum of down-closes over a window, normalised to −100…+100.

  Up[i]   = max(Close[i] − Close[i-1], 0)
  Down[i] = max(Close[i-1] − Close[i], 0)
  CMO(n)  = 100 × (ΣUp − ΣDown) / (ΣUp + ΣDown)   over n bars (default 14)
  Signal  = SMA(9, CMO)   (smoothed line for crossover detection)

Score (0–100):
  35 pts  CMO > Signal (momentum above smoothed line)
  35 pts  CMO > 0 (net positive momentum)
  30 pts  percentile rank of CMO in 252-bar rolling window × 30
