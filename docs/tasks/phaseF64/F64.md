# Task F.64 — Price Volume Trend (PVT) Panel

## Overview
Price Volume Trend — cumulative volume-weighted price change momentum indicator.

  PVT     = Σ ((Close[i] − Close[i-1]) / Close[i-1]) × Volume[i]   (cumulative)
  Signal  = SMA(21, PVT)   (trend confirmation line)
  Slope   = (PVT − PVT[5 bars ago]) / 5   (momentum direction)

Score (0–100):
  40 pts  PVT above Signal line (accumulation dominating)
  30 pts  Slope > 0 (PVT rising)
  30 pts  percentile rank of Slope in 252-bar window
